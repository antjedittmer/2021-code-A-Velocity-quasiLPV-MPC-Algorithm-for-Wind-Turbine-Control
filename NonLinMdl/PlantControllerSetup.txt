Plant and controller setup of the qLMPC / PI comparison on Mdl2
(runGeneratePicsPaper, runCompareCtrl with plant 'SimulinkPaper')
=====================================================================

Plant models (WECS model of the test models)
--------------------------------------------
- Mdl_BianchiOL: Mdl2 (rotor, drivetrain, tower, blade flap, actuators).
  The aerodynamics use the hub-height (freestream) wind V directly.
- Mdl_BianchiOL_WindFiltered: the same model, but the wind passes through
  the rotor-effective wind filter (Lib_WindFilter, end of chapter 3 of the
  thesis) before the aerodynamic lookup tables:
  * projection onto the rotor axis: V_in = cos(5 deg) * V
    (wecs.kWindEffFilt in the paper version)
  * first-order low-pass filters with the wind-dependent time constant
    T = k*R/V_in: torque channel k_tau = 0.75 (kVeFilt_tau),
    thrust channel k_T = 0.25 (kVeFilt)
  * the filter is bypassed for jumps larger than step_thresshold between two
    samples (wind steps of the sweep); step_thresshold = 0 bypasses it
    completely (the cos(5 deg) projection remains)

Controllers
-----------
- PI baseline: does not use the wind. Its test model
  test_SimulinkMdl2_Baseline is saved with Mdl_BianchiOL_WindFiltered; for
  the cases without filter (useFiltered = 0) runCompareCtrl and
  runGeneratePicsPaper set its WECS model to Mdl_BianchiOL in memory.
- qLMPC (design 'paper'): scheduled with the wind at its input. The
  scheduling wind sets the steady-state references Tg_ss, beta_ss and
  omega_ss and the wind increment dV of the prediction model. A wind filter
  at the controller input (time constant R*kVeFiltCtrl/V, never bypassed)
  and a factor kWindEffCtrl before it are available; in the paper design
  kVeFiltCtrl = 0 (no filter) and kWindEffCtrl = 1. Its test model
  test_SimulinkMdl2_qLPVMPCbeta uses Mdl_BianchiOL.

Cases (switch useFiltered in runGeneratePicsPaper / runCompareCtrl)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
0. No filter, useFiltered = 0 (controller comparison, useFilteredCtrl = 0,
   stepped wind sweep and turbulent wind; pitch rate runs 4/8/13 deg/s):
   - PI and qLMPC on the same plant Mdl_BianchiOL (no filter, no cos(5 deg)
     projection), qLMPC scheduled with the same freestream wind
     (PI: OutTableTest2_noFilt.mat, OutTableTest2NTW18_noFilt.mat,
     OutTableTest_rate*_PI_noFilt.mat; qLMPC: OutTableMPC.mat,
     OutTableMPCNTW18.mat, OutTableTest_rate*_MPC.mat)
1. As in the paper, useFiltered = 1 (files with the suffix _old,
   addOldCtrl = 1):
   - PI: Mdl_BianchiOL_WindFiltered; turbulent wind with the filter
     (OutTableTest2NTW18_kVe0dot25_kVeTau0dot75.mat, PI rate runs
     OutTableTest_rate*_PI_1.mat), stepped wind sweep with the filter
     bypassed, i.e. only cos(5 deg) (OutTableTest2.mat)
   - qLMPC: Mdl_BianchiOL without filter, scheduled with the unfiltered wind
   The two controllers therefore run on different plants.
2. Filtered, useFiltered = 2 (turbulent wind, addFilteredCase = 1):
   - PI: Mdl_BianchiOL_WindFiltered with the filter, as in case 1
   - qLMPC: Mdl_BianchiOL_WindFiltered with the same filter, scheduled with
     the filtered wind of the torque channel (kWindEffCtrl = cos(5 deg),
     kVeFiltCtrl = 0.75; setFilteredWindQLMPC)
     (OutTableMPCNTW18_windFilt.mat, OutTableTest_rate*_MPC_windFilt.mat)
   Both controllers run on the same plant, and the qLMPC knows the
   rotor-effective wind of the torque channel exactly (best case; in
   practice it would have to be estimated). The thrust channel uses the
   faster filter (kVeFilt), which the qLMPC does not get.

The pitch rate limit of the controller comparison is set to 13 deg/s in
both data dictionaries (setPitchRateLimitDD).

Pitch rate limits, turbulent wind, case 0: the PI hardly reacts to the rate
limit (4/8/13 deg/s within about 1 %, above 4 deg/s during less than 1 % of
the time); the qLMPC is above 4 deg/s during more than a third of the time
and its tower ratio to the PI at 8 deg/s is 2.58 / 3.82 / 4.14. In case 2
the largest pitch rate is 2.5 deg/s (PI) and 2.6 deg/s (qLMPC), so the
rate limits from 3 deg/s upwards give identical results.
